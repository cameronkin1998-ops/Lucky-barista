# 4 · Content Pillars & Formats

## Pillars and Q4 mix

| Pillar | Share of posts | Funnel job | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Demo & Results** | 30% | See → Click | NanoFoamer turning cold milk into microfoam, then a pour |
| **Gift Ideas** | 25% (rises to 40% in Weeks 4–7) | Click → Buy | A series: "Gift for the coffee person #3: the mug that stirs itself". **One gift per video, one keyword.** |
| **Teach & Fix** | 20% | Care (saves) | "Why your shot is sour: 3 fixes" (Flick, UpShot, Subscale) |
| **Proof & People** | 15% | Buy (trust) | Customer reposts, creator reviews, "what you ordered this week" |
| **Offers & Urgency** | 10% (rises to 25% Nov 24 – Dec 1 and Dec 8–17) | Buy | BFCM reveal, "last day for Christmas shipping" |

Count this ratio across the whole week, not per platform.

## Format by job

| Shape | Job | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Short vertical video (7–45 s) | Reach and demo | TikTok, IG Reels, FB Reels |
| Carousel (4–8 slides) | Saves. One product shown 4 ways (Hook → Product → Detail → Result), with the keyword CTA on the last slide | IG, TikTok photo mode, FB |
| Stories (3–6 frames) | Urgency, polls, link stickers, behind the scenes | IG, FB |
| Live (20–40 min) | Live demo and Q&A, BFCM launch | TikTok Live, IG Live |
| Static product photo + price | Retargeting and catalog | FB, IG feed, ads |
| Pin | Search traffic that keeps working | Pinterest (channel already connected): re-post every hero video |

## Repurposing matrix: one hero shoot becomes 8+ posts

From one 60–90 s raw UGC session on one product:

1. **TikTok:** 15–25 s cut, native text, trending audio
2. **IG Reel:** the same cut with a cleaner caption and a shop tag
3. **FB Reel:** the same cut with a longer caption, posted to the Page
4. **IG/TikTok carousel:** 4 frames from the video (Hook → Product → Detail → Result)
5. **Stories:** 3 frames plus "reply FOAM for the link" (Story replies trigger the bot), plus a poll ("Would you gift this? 🎁 / ☕ for me")
6. **ASMR cut:** 7–10 s, sound only, no talking
7. **Ad version:** the first 3 s re-cut with an offer end-card, held for component 10
8. **Pinterest pin** and an **email GIF** for the newsletter
