# 11 · Measurement & Decisions

## The weekly scorecard (every Friday, 30 min)

Use [`templates/weekly-scorecard.md`](templates/weekly-scorecard.md). Pull the platform numbers from TikTok Studio, IG Insights and Meta Business Suite, and the store numbers using the queries in [component 1](01-baseline-and-goals.md).

| --- | --- | --- |
| See | Views, reach, 3-s hold %, average watch % | Platform insights |

## DM funnel: the most important numbers each week, per keyword

| Step | Metric | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Views | Views on posts featuring the keyword | Platform insights |
| Ask rate | Keyword comments + DMs ÷ views (target ≥ 0.5%) | ManyChat subscribers tagged `dm-<keyword>` this week |
| Sent | DMs delivered by the bot | ManyChat automation stats |
| Tap rate | DM-link sessions ÷ DMs sent (target ≥ 40%) | Shopify sessions where `utm_medium=dm`, `utm_content=<keyword>` |
| Buy rate | Orders ÷ DMs sent (target ≥ 5%) | Shopify orders by UTM / tag `bought-<product>` |

- **Low ask rate:** the hook or CTA is weak. Say the keyword out loud in the video, and put it on screen in the final 2 s.
- **Low tap rate:** Message A is too long, or the link looks suspicious. Shorten it to price + one line.
- **Low buy rate:** price or shipping cost. Test the DM-only discount or a free-shipping threshold.

## Post-level log (one row per post)

`date · platform · product · format# · pillar · source (in-house/creator/AI) · hook text · views · 3s hold % · saves · shares · link clicks · orders (code/UTM) · verdict`

## Decision rules

| Signal | Action |
| --- | --- |
| A format/hook beats median 2 weeks in a row | Make 3 variations next week. Brief creators on it. Promote it to an ad (component 10). |
| A format sits below median 2 weeks in a row | Retire it for the season. |
| A product gets views but no clicks | The hook works but the offer doesn't. Test showing the price on screen, or a bundle. |
| A product gets clicks but no carts | Fix the product page (component 8 checklist) before making more content for it. |
| Carts but no checkouts | Shipping cost or delivery-date doubt. Add a free-shipping threshold or a delivery-by message. |
| A creator's video outperforms | Re-book them within 48 h and ask for 2 more angles. |
| Inventory ≤ 3 units | Swap the product out of Tier A. Use real "almost gone" messaging. |

## Checkpoints

- **Mon Nov 16:** is social traffic above 50 sessions a week? If not, double the TikTok volume and the creator count.
- **Tue Dec 1:** BFCM review. Revenue by channel, best creatives, list growth. Reset the December goals.
- **Mon Jan 11:** season review. What sold, which formats won, the creator roster to keep, and the Q1 plan.
