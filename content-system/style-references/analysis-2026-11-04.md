## What they have in common (so far)

1. **No one talks.** All three rely on music or song plus visuals. There's no voiceover and no face. → **We can make these with hands only, which suits AI generation and the existing Higgsfield recipe.**
2. **The product is doing something in every shot.** Pouring, dripping, inserting, pressing. Nothing is just sitting still being shown off.
3. **Clear glass + liquid in motion** (REF-01/02). Seeing coffee move through clear glass is the hook. It's satisfying and makes people watch again, which is why the shares are high (2.5–3 K).
4. **A calm, cozy-morning mood** (REF-01/02): natural light, a clean counter, slow pacing, a "ritual" framing ("meditative morning"). 
5. **A short ending that gives a payoff**: a finished, café-looking drink (REF-02 adds milk and chocolate) or a question that invites comments (REF-03, "Who needs one?").
6. **Gift framing + seasonal audio** (REF-03): a holiday song and 🎁🎄 in the first line. That's exactly the Q4 angle.
7. **Short:** 7–22 s. Fine for replays.
8. **Hardly any branding**: one caption line at most, and no logos on screen.
