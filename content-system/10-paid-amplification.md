# 10 · Paid Amplification

Organic posting finds out what works. Paid spend scales it. Never put money behind a post that hasn't already proven itself organically.

## When a post earns a boost

A post becomes an ad when, **within 72 h**, it beats the account's 4-week median on two of these:
- 3-second hold rate (or views ÷ reach) ≥ 1.5× median
- Saves + shares ≥ 2× median
- Link clicks / profile visits ≥ 2× median

## Ad structure (Meta)

- **Retargeting ad set:** 30-day site visitors (no purchase) + 30-day IG/FB engagers + 50% video viewers. Creative: UGC review + offer end-card, plus a dynamic catalog carousel.
- **Prospecting ad set:** Advantage+ audience. 3–5 UGC creatives, swapping out losers every 5–7 days.
- Run **partnership ads** with creator handles (requires whitelisting in the creator's agreement).
