# 3 · UGC Style Standard

Most of what we publish is UGC: content that looks and feels made by a real person at a real counter, not by a brand. This file is the standard. It gets locked in once Cameron's reference examples are added to [`style-references/`](style-references/). Until then, these are the starting defaults.

## The look

| Element | Standard |
| --- | --- |
| Camera | Phone, vertical 9:16, 1080×1920. Handheld or propped on the counter. No gimbal-smooth "ad" look. |
| Setting | A real home kitchen or coffee corner. Morning window light is best. A little lived-in clutter is fine. |
| People | Hands always. Face optional. A face plus talking to camera works best for "review" and "POV" formats. |
| Sound | Real sound first: grinder, steam, pour, clink. Trending audio sits quietly under it. Voiceover sounds like texting a friend. |
| Text on screen | The hook in the first second, in native platform text style, inside the safe zone (not the bottom 20% or the right 15%). |
| Length | 7–20 s for demos and trends. 25–45 s for tutorials, reviews and "what I got". |
| Pacing | Something visibly changes every 1–2 seconds. The payoff arrives before second 3 or gets teased in the hook. |
| Branding | No logo bumpers or intros. The product is the brand. Say "Lucky Barista" out loud, or show the box once at most. |

## The voice

- Talk like a friend who is a little obsessed with coffee, not like a store.
- Say specific things: "18 grams in, 36 out, 28 seconds", not "perfect espresso".
- Be honest about the downsides. "Not a La Marzocco, but for $349…" builds more trust than hype.
- **Don't say:** "best-selling", "#1", "revolutionary", or invent customer quotes. See [12-guardrails.md](12-guardrails.md).

## The 8 UGC formats we standardize on

| # | Format | Structure | Best for | Persona |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **POV / morning routine** | "POV: …" hook → 5–8 quick routine shots with the product central → drink in hand | Machines, mugs, foamers | Starter, Self-gifter |
| 2 | **Problem → fix** | Show the fail (flat foam, sputtering shot) → "the fix" → the result, filmed from the same angle | Foamers, FlowTip, WDT, UpShot | Upgrader |
| 3 | **Unboxing + first use** | Box on the counter → open → first drink → honest one-line verdict | Machines, bundles, gifts | Gift buyer, Starter |
| 4 | **Honest review (talking head)** | "I've used X for 30 days. 3 things I love, 1 I don't." → b-roll for each point | Machines, NanoFoamer PRO | Starter |
| 5 | **Gift series ("gift for the coffee person #N")** | Face or voice → ONE product, its payoff shot, price on screen → "comment MUG and I'll send it" | Every impulse product | Gift buyer |
| 6 | **Café vs home math** | "$6.50 a day = $2,372 a year" → make the same drink at home → cost per cup | Machines, foamers | Starter |
| 7 | **ASMR / satisfying** | No talking. Macro sound and texture: pour, tamp, foam swirl, auto-stir | All. Good fillers that get shared | All |
| 8 | **Reply-to-comment / Q&A** | Pin the viewer's question → answer it by demonstrating | Anything people ask about | All |

## Hook bank (first 1–2 seconds, on screen and spoken)

- "The $60 gadget that made me stop buying lattes"
- "If your milk foam looks like this, watch this"
- "Gifts for the coffee person who has everything — under $50"
- "I tested a $349 espresso machine for 30 days"
- "POV: you finally made latte art at home"
- "Things in my coffee corner that are actually worth it"
- "Stop buying this, buy this instead" (café latte vs. a home foamer)
- "Espresso… at a campsite?"
- "My partner said this was the best gift last Christmas" — **only use this when it's the creator's own true story**

## Approval checklist (every UGC piece)

- [ ] The hook lands in ≤ 1.5 s. Text is in the safe zone.
- [ ] The product is clearly visible and used for real (not just held).
- [ ] Every claim is literally true for this product.
- [ ] Sound is clean. Captions or subtitles are on.
- [ ] The creator has signed usage rights (whitelisting, if we'll run it as an ad).
- [ ] Disclosure is set: paid partnership label, "#ad", or "AI-generated" where it applies.
- [ ] One product, one keyword CTA. Say it out loud and show it on screen in the last 2 s ("Comment FOAM"; on TikTok "DM FOAM / tap the cart"). The ManyChat bot is live for that keyword.
