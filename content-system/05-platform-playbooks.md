# 5 · Platform Playbooks

Same footage, different packaging for each platform.

## TikTok (@luckybarista_): discovery engine

| | Standard |
| --- | --- |
| Role | Reach new people. Find out which hooks and products work. |
| Cadence | 1 post a day (minimum 5 a week). 1 Live a week from Week 3. |
| Best times (Central) | 7–9 a.m., 12–1 p.m., 7–10 p.m. Test, then lock in the top 2 by Week 3. |
| Format | Native text, trending sound at low volume, hook in second 0–1, captions on |
| Caption | Hook line → one line on what it does → CTA → 3–5 hashtags (`#coffeetok #homebarista #espresso #giftideas` + one product tag) |
| Selling | **TikTok Shop channel is already connected in Shopify.** Tag products in every video and turn on the Showcase. Without tags, use "link in bio". |
| Affiliates | Open a TikTok Shop affiliate plan (10–20% commission) for Tier A products. This is the cheapest way to scale UGC. |
| Engagement | Reply to comments **with videos** (format 8). Pin the best comment. Stitch or Duet coffee trends. |
| Disclosure | Turn on "AI-generated content" for Higgsfield output, and the "Branded content / your brand" toggle (already in the TikTok skill). |

**Fix to the existing TikTok skill:** its rotation mostly features *coming-soon* items and machines. It should rotate the **impulse lineup** in component 2 (FOAM, SHOT, FLICK, FROTH, MUG), with at most 1 waitlist post a week, and end every caption with "tap the cart 🛒 or DM me <KEYWORD>".

**Getting the link to people on TikTok:** (1) a TikTok Shop product tag on every video, (2) a DM keyword reply in Business Suite, (3) a pinned comment: "DM FOAM for the link ☕". TikTok comments can't trigger DMs automatically, so the CTA says **DM**, not "comment". Details are in [dm-bot/](dm-bot/README.md).

## Instagram: trust and catalog

| | Standard |
| --- | --- |
| Role | Turn interest into trust. The "store window". Saves and shares. |
| Cadence | 5 Reels + 3 carousels a week. Stories every day. |
| Best times (Central) | 7–9 a.m., 11 a.m.–1 p.m., 7–9 p.m. |
| Reels | Same cut as TikTok, re-uploaded without the TikTok watermark. Tag products from the Facebook/IG Shop catalog (channel already connected). Use a trial Reel for new hooks. |
| Carousels | Gift guides and "3 fixes" posts. Slide 1 is the hook, the last slide is the CTA plus "save for later". |
| Stories | Daily: 1 behind the scenes, 1 product with a link sticker, 1 poll or question box. **Pinned Highlights:** Gift Guide · Latte Art · Machines · Reviews · Shipping. |
| Bio | One line on what we are, "🎁 Holiday gift guide ↓", and the link-in-bio (component 8) |
| Collabs | Use **Collab posts** with UGC creators so the Reel shows on both accounts |
| DM automation | **The main CTA on every post:** "Comment **FOAM** and I'll DM you the link". The GHL bot replies publicly and sends the one-tap checkout link ([dm-bot/](dm-bot/README.md)). The bio link points to the current week's main product. |

## Facebook: gift buyers and retargeting

| | Standard |
| --- | --- |
| Role | Reach the **gift buyer** (older and more likely to buy) and run all paid retargeting. |
| Cadence | 5 posts a week (Reels plus 1 to 2 link posts). Stories 3 days a week. |
| Best times (Central) | 8–10 a.m. and 6–8 p.m. Weekends perform well for gifts. |
| Content | Gift guides and "what to get the coffee lover" do best here. Use longer captions with prices. Say "Shop now" plainly. |
| Shop | The Facebook sales channel is connected. Check that the Shop shows **only active products**, organized into the gift collections. |
| Groups | 30 minutes a week of genuinely helpful answers in home-espresso and coffee groups. Only link when someone asks. |
| Messenger | The same GHL comment-to-DM bot as Instagram (Facebook comment trigger). "Comment FOAM" works on FB Reels and posts. Turn on instant replies for "shipping" and "gift". |
| Ads | This is the home of retargeting (component 10). The Facebook Ads connector in Claude **needs authorization** in claude.ai connector settings before Claude can read or adjust campaigns. |
