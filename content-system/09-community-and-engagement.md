# 9 · Community & Engagement

Posting is half the job. The other half is what happens in the first hour after a post goes live, and in the DMs.

## Response standards

| Item | Standard |
| --- | --- |
| Comments in the first 60 min after posting | Reply to **every** comment. This tells the algorithm the post is getting engagement. |
| Comment reply time (8 a.m.–9 p.m.) | Under 2 h |
| DM reply time | Under 2 h. Buying questions ("does it ship by…", "which machine") get answered first. |
| Negative comments | Reply once, publicly, with facts. Then offer a DM. Never delete honest criticism; only hide spam or abuse. |
| Question comments | Turn the best 2 a week into **reply videos** (format 8) |

## DM keywords (run by the bot, see [dm-bot/](dm-bot/README.md))

| Keyword | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| FOAM, SHOT, FLICK, FROTH, MUG, TEMP, SCREEN | The product DM with a one-tap checkout link ([products.csv](dm-bot/products.csv)) |
| "link", "want", "price" (no product named) | The bot asks which product, then sends that link |
| EARLY | Tag `bfcm-early`, ask for an email, early access on Nov 24 |
| MACHINE | Hand off to Cameron: "Tell me your budget, and do you drink milk drinks or straight espresso?" |
| STOP | Opt out, end all automations |
| Questions, complaints, order issues | The bot doesn't send a link. Cameron gets the handoff and replies personally within 2 h. |

**Cameron's daily check (10 min):** the GHL inbox, filtered to "assigned to me". These are the conversations the bot handed off.

## The UGC loop (customers making content for us)

1. A post-purchase email on day 10: "Show us your setup → tag @luckybarista_ for a chance to be featured, and get $10 off your next order."
2. Search tags and mentions every day. Ask permission in the DM before reposting ("Can we share this? We'll credit you").
3. Repost to Stories the same day. Feature the best one in a weekly "your setups" post.
4. Log every piece of customer UGC with its permission status in the scorecard.

## Community participation (30 min a week)

- Facebook groups and Reddit (r/espresso, r/homebarista): answer questions helpfully, with no links unless someone asks. Follow each community's rules.
- Comment on 10 larger coffee creators' posts each week (on topic, adding something useful, not "great post!").
- Stitch or Duet 1 trending coffee video a week on TikTok.
