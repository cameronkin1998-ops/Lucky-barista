# 12 · Guardrails

These apply to every post, ad, brief and DM.

## Honesty & claims
- Only describe what a product literally does, using its specs. No invented features.
- No "best-selling", "#1", "award-winning" or "as seen on" unless it's documented. (The H13's "French design award" comes from the manufacturer's listing. Name the source or leave it out.)
- No invented reviews, testimonials, sales numbers or "X people bought this today".
- Scarcity and countdowns have to be real. Discounts have to come off a genuine prior price.

## Disclosure (FTC + platform rules)
- Paid or gifted creators **have to** disclose: the platform's paid-partnership toggle, plus `#ad` or `#sponsored` early in the caption. Put this in every agreement.
- Affiliates disclose their commission ("I earn a commission").
- AI-generated media (Higgsfield) gets the platform's **AI-generated label** (TikTok `is_aigc`, Meta "AI info"). AI content is never presented as a real customer or a real person's experience. **No AI "UGC actors" giving testimonials.**

## Rights
- Get written usage rights before reposting customer content (a DM "yes" is the minimum; save a screenshot).
- Creator agreements state: organic use, paid-ad whitelisting duration (90 days by default), editing rights, exclusivity (if any), and the payment date.
- Use only licensed or platform-library music. Business accounts must use the **commercial music library** on TikTok and Meta.

## Operational
- Drafts only for automated TikTok posts until Cameron changes the skill (the current skill setting).
- Don't promise holiday delivery dates beyond the carrier cutoff Cameron confirms.
- Never post customer order details, addresses or names without consent.
